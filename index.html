About me

I'm a machine-learning engineer who likes getting models out of the notebook and into something people can actually use — computer vision, NLP and LLM pipelines wrapped in APIs that hold up in production. I recently finished an MS in Computer Science at the University of Kentucky, where my work sat at the intersection of deep learning, medical imaging and genomics.

My route here ran through UET Lahore (BS Computer Science, top 3 of the department), a US State Department Global UGRAD exchange semester at Fayetteville State University, and industry R&D work at AxcelerateAI and Omdena. Along the way I've taught Data Structures & Algorithms labs, run hands-on ML workshops, and served as a Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador.

Lately I've been deep in LLM retrieval systems — semantic ranking with pgvector, two-stage retrieval, evaluation harnesses — and in applying self-supervised learning to medical imaging, where labels are scarce and expensive. I'm currently open to ML and AI engineering roles.
